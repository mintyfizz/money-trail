About these numbers

What is counted

Every prime contract and financial assistance transaction in Treasury's national archives for each loaded fiscal year. The figure is net obligations: money agencies legally committed, minus money they took back. It is not money paid out. Loans are the exception: they count at their subsidy cost, the government's expected cost, as on USAspending; their face value is shown beside it and never added.

Place means where the work is performed or the money is used, not where the recipient is based. A Virginia contractor building a ship in Maine counts for Maine.

Agencies are the awarding agency. Spending types follow Treasury's award type codes; a type Treasury marks as not specified is kept as its own group.

Limits

  • The Congressional Research Service, citing the Government Accountability Office, warns that the source data may be incomplete and inaccurate.
  • Recipient rankings exclude aggregate records, records redacted for privacy and transactions without a recipient ID; their total is shown beside the ranking.
  • Subawards are not included.
  • Amounts are nominal dollars, not adjusted for inflation.
  • Per-resident figures divide by the latest state population USAspending publishes, not the population in each fiscal year.
  • Some aggregate records, such as Medicare payments, are placed where the reporting contractor is, not where each beneficiary lives.
