About these numbers

What is counted

Every prime contract transaction in Treasury's national archive for each fiscal year, including indefinite-delivery vehicles. The figure is net obligations: money agencies legally committed, minus money they took back. It is not money paid out.

Place means where the work is performed, not where the company is based. A Virginia contractor building a ship in Maine counts for Maine.

Limits

  • Treasury and the Government Accountability Office report that the source data is incomplete and contains errors.
  • Grants, loans and other assistance are not included yet.
  • Amounts are nominal dollars, not adjusted for inflation.
  • Per-resident figures use the state populations USAspending publishes.
